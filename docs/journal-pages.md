# Journal pages and workflows

**Meal journal round:** Custom starts with a yellow searchable/filterable Meal diary, keeps saved Meals and Recipes, adds the blue selected-day/selected-meal planner below Recipes, and (only when enabled in Settings > Today & pages) a red Photo diary. Plans, photos and optional diary notes are integrated into Today and Add food. The desktop layout gains a responsive sidebar and Custom creation menu; the original mobile header/dock are retained. `?view=app&tab=custom&intro=0` opens Custom directly without the tour or launch intro. Workflows, storage limits and file roles: [meal-journal.md](meal-journal.md).

> **Scope:** What each page does: Today, Food, Movement, Custom, Statistics, Period, Day overview, Settings and Profile.
>
> **Read when:** you change page behaviour or need the intended UX of a page/dialog.
>
> **Related:** journal-shell.md, calculations-colors.md, data-off-catalogue.md, file-map/README.md

- **Today:** day arrows/calendar, Intake/Burned and net balance, macros, optional sugar immediately to the right of salt, six meal slots, and collapsible activity, water, bowel and custom trackers. The Compact/Normal/Expanded switch sits centred under the macros and shows **only its three icons** (no text; the names stay as accessible labels and tooltips); it and the open states are remembered. Settings controls the Today dock, not the fixed lists of widget shortcuts.
- **Food:** multilingual fuzzy search, favorites (while typing, only favourites that directly match the text, i.e. every word is an exact/prefix/substring hit, are pinned above the other results; favourites that match only loosely keep their normal rank; with no text they are all listed first), remembered portions, multi-item queues, saved meals and previous-day copying. Search covers 410 bundled foods (curated products, generic foods and USDA entries, one entry per food: the estimated "fried / baked / grilled / boiled …" variants that used to be generated from every generic food were removed), your own records, and an offline catalogue of **130,000 Open Food Facts products**. Every result shows the weight that will be pre-filled (the last weight you logged for it, else its classic portion when the data has one, else 100 g/ml) with the carbs, protein, fat and kcal of exactly that weight; picking it opens the quantity screen with that same weight. Its main **Add another item** button queues the food and returns to the picker; saved meals and previous-day meals join the same batch instead of logging and closing the dialog. The journal is only written when you press **Log all**, which appears after the first item. New unknown barcode searches are the only automatic HTTP fetch made by a logging workflow, and only after the user requests a lookup.
- **Movement:** Walk/Run by distance or duration, Other with 181 local Compendium activities, or a saved custom activity. Records retain the estimated burn at logging time. Water, weight, bowel entries and custom quantities are independent daily measurements.
- **Custom:** saved meals, recipes, ingredient quantities, catalog, barcode lookup and manual creation. Meals/recipes support logging, editing, fuzzy search and confirmed deletion. Ingredient searches use the same catalogue, result rows and default weight as Add Food; adding an ingredient queues it and offers another by default, with **Save meal** appearing only after the first ingredient. The chosen ingredient shows its carbs, protein, fat, fibre and salt for the weight, and every listed ingredient shows its own macros and kcal. In Ultra Minimal, section and action labels are reduced while item names and values remain.
- **Statistics:** 7-day, 30-day or custom ranges, energy/nutrient averages (including sugar when enabled), custom tracker charts, weight trend, and a collapsible 7-day breakdown with a sugar total on each day. Ultra Minimal nutrient averages put `/day` beside the number; custom quantity charts use a bottom-right per-day label.
- **Period:** optional calendar, daily flow/mood/symptom/notes, pill schedule, intercourse log, cycle history and opt-in nutrition charts. Profile sex controls default visibility unless overridden. Existing data is not deleted when hiding the page. Estimates are not contraception or fertility guidance.
- **Day overview:** single-tap a date in Today's calendar, double-tap a date in Period, or inspect a day from Statistics. The order is energy, macros (if present), Poop (unless hidden), Activity, Weight/Water, then logged custom trackers. Icon backplates blend with their sections. The Open in Today footer stays available while scrolling.
- **Body (optional):** appears left of Statistics when Settings > Advanced > Body measurements is on: range chips (3M / 6M / 1Y / Custom), one graph card per tracked measure and Upper body / Lower body / Custom buttons at the bottom that open a pop-up to update a single body part. Details: [advanced-personalisation.md](advanced-personalisation.md).
- **Goals and limits (optional):** Settings > Advanced; a small check (objective reached) or exclamation (limit reached) icon appears in the Intake, macro, fibre, salt and sugar fields of Today. Details: [advanced-personalisation.md](advanced-personalisation.md).
- **Settings and Profile:** collapsible groups, each with its sub-sections titled when open. **Language** (English/Italian) is now a collapsible group of its own, above Appearance. **Appearance**: app Theme (Light/Lite Dark/Dark), home-screen widget style (Light/Dark) immediately below Theme, Style presets (Classic plus 6 style themes and the Mono hue picker; each preset shows **only its name**, no description), and **Ultra minimal** (the switch used to be labelled "Ultra minimal mode"). **Today & pages**: the **Quick-add buttons** (formerly "Today quick-add"), the three tracking switches in one group in this order: **Track poop, Track period, Track sugar**, and the quick-add food shortcuts. **Custom buttons**: your custom trackers and the *Custom button in widgets* switch, which is disabled until at least one custom tracker exists (and is forced off when the last one is deleted). The quick-add buttons are stacked one per row. Widget style, the widget custom button, Ultra minimal and sugar tracking have labels without explanatory subtitles. The **Advanced** group (below Custom buttons) holds the goals / limits and body-measurement switches, and Profile gets a *Body measurements* section when that switch is on. Then **JSON backup** and the **Install app** card, both **collapsible groups like all the others** (the *Browse food / Foods database* shortcut was removed from Settings; the Foods page itself is unchanged). The Install card is only visible in a browser, never in the Android APK, see [pwa.md](pwa.md) and, at the very bottom, a small, muted red **Delete all data** button (no title or description; translucent red tint, centred, not full width): it asks twice (two successive confirmation dialogs, `DeleteAllData.tsx`), then erases the whole local storage (and, on Android, every pending reminder) and reloads the app empty. In Ultra minimal the expanded **Activity** card lists each activity with its visible name (type) and kcal; only the duration/distance detail line is hidden there. In Ultra Minimal, salt is the supplied nested diamond with four spokes in Today, Statistics and Custom. Its accessible name still identifies salt.

**Aurora style note:** its pill radius (999px) is for chips and buttons only; Custom page cards and ingredient lists, Period cards, calendar days and day-overview rows use 14-18 px corners (overrides at the end of `src/index.css`), so they are no longer stretched into pill shapes.

**Meal and recipe editors (Custom page).** The ingredient search sits **above** the ingredients already in the meal / recipe (so it stays at the top when editing an existing one). It behaves like Add Food: results show the favourite star on every row, favourites that directly match the typed text are pinned first (`pinCoherentFavorites`), and typing one character lists the favourites that start with it (`matchesFirstChar`). All in `src/components/prebuilt/PreBuiltMealsView.tsx` (`filteredFoodsForSearch`, `recipeResults`, `favoriteOneChar`); `FoodResultRow` gets `favorite`.
- **Advanced layout (optional):** Settings > Advanced; a pencil on Today, Statistics and Body lets you move, resize and remove fields, and add meal types (Today) or measures (Body); added meal types appear in every meal selection. Details: [advanced-layout.md](advanced-layout.md).

**Meals, add food and nutrient fields (latest round).** Expanding a meal on Today lists its items, and each item now shows its own carbs, protein, fat, fibre, salt (and sugar when tracking is on) for the portion eaten (`FoodMacroGrid` with `dense`, scaled to the item's current amount, also while its weight is being edited). **Add food** (`AddFoodModal.tsx`): when foods are waiting in the batch, or a food is picked but not added, any dismissal (tap outside, swipe down, X, Back) opens `SaveBeforeCloseDialog` (Save / Don't save / Keep editing; EN + IT strings inside the component) instead of closing; the dialog keeps its state while it is asked. **Today fibre / salt / sugar fields** (`SecondaryNutrientCard`): name above the number, both centred like the macro cards, goal badge in the top-right corner (icon above the number in Ultra minimal); this also applies to the Statistics averages.

**Settings (Today & pages / Custom buttons):** *Quick-add buttons* has a collapse arrow; *Track poop*, *Track period* and *Track sugar* are three switches in one group, in that order (on = tracked; poop and period are stored inverted as `hidePoop` / `hidePeriod`); **Delete all data** is a full-width button as wide as the other Settings fields; *Quick-add food shortcuts* has no hint text; in *Custom buttons* the home-screen widget switch is below the *New custom button* button.

**Meal totals (latest round).** An expanded meal on Today now starts with a row of three yellow fields with the **whole meal's** fibre, salt and sugar (sugar only when *Track sugar* is on; icon + number in Compact), followed by the items, each with its own values. Carbs / protein / fat of the meal stay in the header pills. Component: `src/components/dashboard/MealNutrientTotals.tsx`.

**Meal totals theme fix.** The fibre / salt / sugar fields of an expanded meal now carry `data-nutrient`, so every style (Angular, Aurora, Pop, Playful, Mono, ...) colours, rounds, outlines and shadows them like the other yellow fields, and their icons (wheat, salt crystal, candy) follow the theme's text colour. Icons are 15 px, the same as the per-item fields.
- **Meal chosen by the time of day:** Add food, the barcode and custom-food popups and the Foods / Custom pages preselect the meal that fits the clock, and the user can still tap another meal. Windows (local time): breakfast 05:00-09:59, morning snack 10:00-11:29, lunch 11:30-14:59, afternoon snack 15:00-17:59, dinner 18:00-22:29, extra 22:30-04:59 (`MEAL_TIME_WINDOWS` in `src/utils/mealByTime.ts`). If that meal was removed with the advanced layout, the closest built-in meal in Today order is used. It applies to the Add food button of the bottom bar, the widget / tour *food* action (`App.tsx`), the Foods page (log popup, barcode, custom food), and the Custom page (barcode, custom food, *Foods*, the default meal of a new saved meal, the log popups of saved meals without a meal tag and of recipes). It does **not** apply to the **+** of a meal card on Today: that one always opens its own meal. Saved meals that have a meal tag still log to that tag. `useAutoMealSlot()` (`src/utils/mealSlots.ts`) returns the function that computes it.

**Meal field: nutrients next to the macros, Ultra minimal without units (latest round).** The whole-meal **fibre, salt and sugar** (sugar only with *Track sugar*) are no longer a separate row under a dividing line inside the expanded meal. They are now part of the meal card's header, in the **same area as the carbs / protein / fat pills**: a second row of pills directly under them (aligned to the same columns in Normal), visible whether the meal is open or closed, with no border between them. They show **only the icon and the number** (no "Fibre / Salt / Sugar" text; the name is the `title` / `aria-label`), in every style. In the Normal layout the number carries a `g`; in **Compact and Ultra minimal there is no unit**. The pills carry `data-macro="fiber|salt|sugar"`, so each style colours them like its macro pills (`index.css`). The items of an expanded meal follow the same rule: in **Ultra minimal** the unit after the amount (`150g` -> `150`), the `kcal` after the item energy and the `g` in each item's nutrient grid (`FoodMacroGrid mode="minimal"`) are removed. The amount editor that opens when you tap an item keeps its unit, because it is an input. See [file-map/components-dashboard.md](file-map/components-dashboard.md).

**Polish round (Log food / water, edge-fade, stepper).** *Add food* / *Add water* are now **Log food** / **Log water** (title and meal-card button; Italian *Registra*), and the water confirmation button reads **Save water**. The water pop-up shows the amount stepper first and the quick buttons **below** it (*Glass 250* became **250ml**). The Today dock's food button is a plain fork and knife (not crossed; also in Settings > Quick-add buttons) and the Intake card icon (Ultra minimal) is an arrow. In the poop pop-up *(optional)* sits right next to **Bristol type**. *Save before closing?* has no bin icon on *Don't save*. Settings > Advanced > Goals & limits shows its items **indented under a guide line** so they read as sub-sections. In the Angular style the meal selection chips (Add food, Custom meals) are chamfered like the other controls. **Edge-fade:** every scrollable field with hidden content (pop-up bodies, search result lists, chip rows) fades the edge that still has content, and the fade goes away at the end (`src/utils/scrollFade.ts`, `[data-scroll-fade]` in `index.css`). **Food portion stepper:** after a **+**, a **-** steps back by only 10 g above 100 g and 20 g above 300 g (never below 100 / 300 g); a plain **-** without a **+** before keeps the usual steps (see [file-map/components-common.md](file-map/components-common.md)).

**Tidy-up round.** On Today, a meal's fibre / salt / sugar pills appear only while the meal is open (they slide with the meal's accordion); the Compact / Normal / Expanded switch is right-aligned and smaller; the Intake icon (Ultra minimal) is a tilted arrow (`ArrowDownLeft`), no longer a download-style arrow. On Statistics the "n/n days logged" line is hidden when every day of the period is logged (7/7, 30/30...). Every page change by tap gives the fields a barely visible rise-and-fade ([motion.md](motion.md)).
