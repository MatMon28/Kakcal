# Advanced rounds (moved from the root README)

> **Scope:** Round-by-round notes for Advanced personalisation and the Advanced layout editor (goals / limits, Body page, pencil editor, Settings reorganisation).
>
> **Read when:** you need the history of a Settings > Advanced, Body page or layout-editor change. For current behaviour open [../advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md) or [../advanced-personalisation.md](../advanced-personalisation.md).
>
> **Related:** [../advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md), [../advanced-personalisation.md](../advanced-personalisation.md), [../journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md)

**Advanced personalisation (Settings > Advanced):** opt-in daily *objectives/limits* for calories, fibre, carbs, protein, fat, salt and sugar (a small check/exclamation icon appears in the field on Today when reached) and *body measurements* (a Body page left of Statistics with a graph per measure over 3M/6M/1Y/custom, Upper body / Lower body / Custom update buttons, a tracking section in Profile and custom measures). See [docs/advanced-personalisation.md](../advanced-personalisation.md).

**Advanced layout (Settings > Advanced):** an opt-in switch (with a *Restore original layout* button right under it). When on, a small pencil appears at the top of Today, Statistics and Body: pressing it lets you **touch a field to select it** (highlighted border, resize knobs and a remove button appear), **hold it to move it** to any free place (blank space between fields is allowed), **resize it by pulling its sides or corners** (width snaps to the 6 columns, height is free), remove it, bring removed fields back, and on Today add your own meal types, which then also appear in every meal selection (Add food, Custom meals, Foods). See [docs/advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md).

**Advanced round (drag and drop / icons):** the advanced layout is now a real drag-and-drop editor with side and corner resize handles; *Fibre*, *Salt* and *Sugar* are three independent fields on Today and Statistics; the goal / limit badges were redesigned (solid green check circle / red warning triangle, visible on every theme); the body-part icons show the part with its joint, hand or foot (flexed arm, forearm + hand, bent leg, calf + foot, simplified neck); the profile icon in Settings > Advanced > Body measurements is centred with its text; *Delete all data* is smaller and quieter. See [docs/advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md) and [docs/advanced-personalisation.md](../advanced-personalisation.md).

**Free-placement round (touch to select, long press to move):** the advanced layout has no move buttons and no dashed outlines any more. Touching a field highlights its border and shows its resize knobs and the remove button (touching blank space or another field clears it); holding a field lifts it and it can be dropped on any free cell of the 6-column grid, so blank space can sit between fields (a bar shows when a new row is inserted instead). Meal fields can be shrunk down to their icon, expand arrow and + (minimum 3 columns, 2 in Compact, x 56 px), and pressing the expand arrow of a shrunk meal restores its classic size. The **Medium widget** no longer shows icons on its intake / burned cards (native layouts and gallery preview). New file `src/utils/layoutGrid.ts`; see [docs/advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md) and [docs/widgets.md](../widgets.md).

**Edit round (dashed outlines, free shapes, expand returns to the chosen shape):** the pencil now *edits* the page (the words "Personalise / Personalising" became "Edit / Editing", also in Italian). While editing, every field has a **dashed (segmented) outline**; the selected field gets a **solid border that is identical on all four sides**; the remove **X sits in the top-right corner** of the field instead of its middle. Fields are placed at **any height** on the 6-column canvas (not on rows any more), so you can build shapes like a tall field with two small ones stacked beside it, a pyramid, or staggered fields; dropped fields snap magnetically to their neighbours and a field that would overlap another pushes it down. Expanding a meal shows it in its classic form, collapsing it brings back the shape you chose. Layouts saved by the previous version are converted automatically. No new files, no `package.json` change; see [docs/advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md).

**Polish round (icons, badges, widget pill, meal items, save prompt):** the goal / limit badges now follow the style and theme (outline in the card's own ink tinted green / red, container shape per style); the neck icon has narrower shoulders, the chest icon is a V-shaped torso with two pectorals, the biceps tape sits on the muscle and the forearm icon is a fist on a widening forearm (no longer a wrist); the Small / Extra-small widget pill in *Minimal* is a crisp hairline-bordered slate tray instead of the soft cream pill; every item of an expanded meal shows its own macros, fibre, salt and sugar; dismissing *Add food* with unsaved foods asks *Save / Don't save / Keep editing* (new `SaveBeforeCloseDialog.tsx`); Today's fibre / salt / sugar fields have the name above the number, centred like the macros; the calorie-surplus pill on Intake no longer uses the red deficit colours in the non-classic styles (`--intake-badge-*`). See [docs/advanced-personalisation.md](../advanced-personalisation.md), [docs/widgets.md](../widgets.md), [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md) and [docs/calculations-colors.md](../calculations-colors.md).

**Quick-add buttons / pencil round (settings order, editor fixes):** the Settings row formerly called *Today quick-add* is now **Quick-add buttons** (Italian: *Pulsanti di aggiunta rapida*); **Track sugar** moved into the same group as the other tracking switches, in the order **Track poop, Track period, Track sugar**; the **Delete all data** button is as wide as the other Settings fields; with Advanced layout on, the **pencil on Statistics and Body sits next to the time-period selector** (and returns to the top of the page if that field is removed). Testing the layout editor found and fixed: the remove X / reset button / resize knobs being cut in half on full-width fields, the outline of a pill-shaped field (Compact / Normal / Expanded) drawn as an ellipse, tiny side-knob hit areas on short fields, and fields below a short one (the period selector) jumping down about 26 px the first time a page was edited. No new files, no `package.json` change. See [docs/advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md), [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md) and [docs/changes-patch.md](../changes-patch.md).

**Settings / layout-editor round (goals, tracking toggles, icons, edit fixes):** *Objective* is now called **Goal** and **every** item (calories, calories burned, fibre, carbs, protein, fat, salt, sugar, water) can be a goal or a limit; the descriptions under *Goals & limits*, *Body measurements* and *Advanced layout* are gone and *Goals & limits* has a collapse arrow. *Hide poop fields* / *Hide Period page* became **Track poop** / **Track period** (switch on = tracked; poop is tracked by default, the period by default unless the profile is male; stored values are still `hidePoop` / `hidePeriod`, so nothing is migrated), *Track sugar intake* became **Track sugar**, the *Quick add* toggles can be collapsed, the text under *Quick-add food shortcuts* is gone, and in *Custom buttons* the widget switch sits below the add button. Quick-add rows whose feature is off (poop off, no custom buttons) are greyed out. In the layout editor the pencil sits in the Today calendar row, the selected outline copies the card's real corner radius (no pills) with uniform round resize dots, resized fields no longer show a scrollbar (the Carbs slider), a second finger can scroll the page while a field is held, empty trackers are not editable fields, a meal's calories disappear when it is resized too narrow, and the Pop-style shadows of the Profile / Settings buttons are no longer clipped. New icons: shorter neck, forearm with an open hand, tape-measure for custom measures. New file `src/components/common/InlineCollapse.tsx`. See [docs/advanced-layout.md](../advanced-layout.md), [docs/advanced-personalisation.md](../advanced-personalisation.md) and [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md).
