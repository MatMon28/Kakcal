# UI and brand rounds (moved from the root README)

> **Scope:** Round-by-round notes for Today / meal-card tweaks, the logo and animations, meal-by-time and polish rounds.
>
> **Read when:** you need the history of a Today, logo, motion or wording change. For current behaviour open [../journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md), [../brand.md](../brand.md) or [../motion.md](../motion.md).
>
> **Related:** [../journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md), [../brand.md](../brand.md), [../motion.md](../motion.md)

**Meal totals round (fibre, salt, sugar in expanded meals):** expanding a meal on Today now shows the whole meal's **fibre, salt and sugar** (sugar when *Track sugar* is on) in three yellow fields above the items, in addition to each item's own values. New file `src/components/dashboard/MealNutrientTotals.tsx`; edited `MealSlotCard.tsx`. No `package.json` change. See [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md) and [docs/file-map/components-dashboard.md](../file-map/components-dashboard.md).

**Meal totals theme fix (fibre / salt / sugar icons and fields):** the three yellow fields at the top of an expanded meal were not picked up by the theme CSS (they only had `data-meal-total`), so their colours, radius, border, shadow and clip-path did not follow the selected style and their icons did not follow the theme's text colour. They now carry `data-nutrient="fiber|salt|sugar"` like the item fields and the Today cards, and their icons use the same size (15) as the per-item fields. Edited `src/components/dashboard/MealNutrientTotals.tsx` only; no new files, no `package.json` change. See [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md) and [docs/file-map/components-dashboard.md](../file-map/components-dashboard.md).

**Logo-click round (animated poop):** tapping the **Kakcal logo** at the top of the page now animates **only the poop** of the wordmark (the K and the letters stay still) and still does what it did before (back to Today, or scroll to the top). Six animations inspired by the launch intro, none of them a copy: *plop* (falls, squashes and bounces), *flush* (spins down a whirlpool and corkscrews back up), *jelly* (squash and stretch wobble), *flip* (crouch, jump, front flip, squashed landing), *press* (pressed like a key, pops with three burst ticks) and *stack* (the tip and three tiers plop down one after another, then a pop). A random one is picked and never the same twice in a row; tapping during an animation restarts with another one; nothing plays with *reduce motion*. `?poop=1` to `?poop=6` forces one for review. New files `src/components/common/poopAnimations.ts` and `poop-anim.css`; edited `Brand.tsx` (marks up the poop) and `Header.tsx` (click handler). No `package.json` change. See [docs/motion.md](../motion.md#logo-click-poop-animations).

**Meal-by-time round (the meal is chosen for you):** opening Add food from the bottom bar or a widget, and the Foods and Custom pages, now preselect the meal that fits the time of day (breakfast 05:00-09:59, morning snack 10:00-11:29, lunch 11:30-14:59, afternoon snack 15:00-17:59, dinner 18:00-22:29, extra otherwise) instead of the last used meal or a fixed *lunch*; every meal picker still lets you change it, the **+** of a meal card still opens its own meal, and a meal hidden in the advanced layout falls back to the closest visible one. New file `src/utils/mealByTime.ts`; edited `mealSlots.ts` (`useAutoMealSlot`), `QuickAddBar.tsx`, `App.tsx`, `FoodDatabaseView.tsx`, `PreBuiltMealsView.tsx`. No `package.json` change. See [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md) and [docs/changes-patch.md](../changes-patch.md).

**New-logo round (icon, wordmark and animations everywhere):** the Kakcal logo is now a **solid poop** (pointed tip and three rounded tiers) with the **K carved out of its belly**; the name is **k + the same poop + kcal** (Nunito Black outlines). It replaces the outlined poop beside a K **everywhere**: icon SVGs, favicon, header and tour wordmark, install guide, launcher / round / adaptive / Play PNGs, PWA icons, the notification glyph (the K is a real hole) and badge, the design-asset logos and the overview sheet. The **six launch animations** are new (*soft-serve, stack & plop, jelly key-press, iris flip, swirl & unfold, flush & return*): they run 2.0-2.4 s on their own, so `INTRO_SPEED` went from 1.35 to **2.5** and they play in 0.80-0.96 s, as long as the old ones (0.79-0.95 s). The **six logo-click animations** are new too (plop, flush, jelly, flip, press, stack; same durations as before, `stack` replaces `sketch`). Everything is generated from one definition file: new `scripts/brand-animations.mjs` and `scripts/generate-poop-animations.mjs`; rewritten `scripts/brand-geometry.mjs`, `generate-icon.mjs`, `outline-wordmark.mjs`, `generate-notification-icon.mjs`, `generate-intro-animations.mjs`; edited `render-brand.mjs`, `render-pwa-icons.mjs`, `smoke.mjs`, `Brand.tsx`, `poopAnimations.ts`, `introAnimations.ts`, `index.css`, `offline-worker.js` (cache `v5`). The six `-lockup.svg` files are gone (the new launch animations are the mark only) and `intro_animation.md` was rewritten. The colours are Koink's exactly (ink `#0B0F1E` on `#F9F3E6`; dark `#F9F3E6` on `#070A14`), the Android notification accent is `#0B0F1E` (`capacitor.config.json`, `notifications.ts`), browser notifications carry the app icon and a white-glyph badge `public/icons/badge-96.png`, and `/icons/*` is `no-cache` in `public/_headers`. Also fixed: `res/values/ic_launcher_background.xml` was empty and now holds `#F9F3E6`. The in-app poop *feature* icons (`PoopIcon`) are unchanged. No `package.json` change. See [docs/brand.md](../brand.md).

**Meal nutrients + left / right round:** in the meal field, **fibre, salt and sugar now sit in the same area as the macros** (a row of pills right under carbs / protein / fat in the card header, no dividing line) and show **only their icons and numbers**, never the name, in every style; in **Ultra minimal** the units (item amount unit, `kcal`, `g`) are gone from an expanded meal too. **Body measurements are recorded for both sides** of biceps, forearms, thighs and calves: Left / Right switch in the update pop-up, one Body card with two chips and a two-line chart (solid + circles = left, dashed + diamonds = right); older single values are migrated to the right side. Edited `MealNutrientTotals.tsx`, `MealSlotCard.tsx`, `index.css`, `BodyView.tsx`, `BodyChart.tsx`, `BodyMeasureModal.tsx`, `BodyMeasuresProfile.tsx`, `advancedStore.ts`, `types/advanced.ts`, `i18n/advanced.ts`; no new files, no `package.json` change. See [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md) and [docs/advanced-personalisation.md](../advanced-personalisation.md#left-and-right-side-latest-round).

**Polish round (Log food / water, edge-fade, dynamic minus):** *Add food* / *Add water* became **Log food** / **Log water** and the water confirmation is **Save water**; the water pop-up puts the stepper first and the quick buttons below it (**250ml**); the Today dock food button is a non-crossed fork and knife and the Intake icon is an arrow; Settings: Language, Backup and Install are collapsible like the other groups, the Foods database shortcut and the style descriptions are gone, and Goals & limits items are indented as sub-sections; the Compact / Normal / Expanded switch shows only icons; Bristol type has *(optional)* right next to it; *Don't save* has no bin; the Angular style's meal chips are no longer rounded; scrollable fields with hidden content get an **edge-fade** (new `src/utils/scrollFade.ts`); and in the food portion stepper a **-** after a **+** steps back by 10 g above 100 g and 20 g above 300 g. See [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md) and [docs/changes-patch.md](../changes-patch.md).

**Tidy-up round (meal pills, logged days, layout switch, intake icon, page-change motion):** fibre / salt / sugar of a meal show only when the meal is open; the "n/n days logged" text is hidden when all days of the period are logged; the Compact / Normal / Expanded switch moved to the right and is smaller; the Intake icon is tilted (`ArrowDownLeft`); every page change gives the fields a barely noticeable rise-and-fade (new `src/utils/pageFields.ts`) and the page slide was retuned to 10 px / 180 ms. See [docs/motion.md](../motion.md) and [docs/journal-pages.md](../journal-pages.md).

**Android handoff:** picture ZIPs and readable plan files use the existing native document-picker flow (rebuild the APK). Native share links target the repository's existing public homepage, `https://matmon28.github.io/Kakcal/`; self-hosters can set `VITE_PUBLIC_APP_URL`. This adds no remote API.

**Page-transition round (tap / Back page change):** the old page used to vanish at once, the window jumped to the top and the new page's fields then rose in one by one on top of a 10 px rail slide. It is now one fade through: the old page stays in place and fades out (110 ms) while the new page fades in and slides 14 px (240 ms, 60 ms later). `src/utils/pageFields.ts` and the `data-static-fields` attribute were removed; `src/utils/pageTransition.ts` and `scripts/check-page-transition.mjs` were added. See [../motion.md](../motion.md).

**Page-change performance round:** opening Custom (and going back to Today) froze the app for up to ~1.3 s (4x CPU throttle) when much data was loaded, because the page was mounted with every collapsed meal / recipe card fully built, `Tabs` forced a whole-document layout after every render, ingredients were looked up with `allFoods.find`, and the Meal diary re-filtered the whole history on every render. Collapsed bodies are now mounted on first open (`LazyBody`), lookups use `Map`s, the Tabs measurement runs only when labels change, collapsed off-screen cards use `content-visibility`. Today -> Custom ~1.1 s -> ~0.3 s. New `scripts/bench-page-change.mjs`. See [../performance.md](../performance.md).
