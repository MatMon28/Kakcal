# Styles guide and requirements

> **Scope:** complete specification of all nine Kakcal theme styles (`classic`, `angular`, `aurora`, `pop`, `playful`, `minimal`, `mono`, `glass`, `material`), how each renders across Light / Lite Dark / Dark, and the mandatory requirements any new or edited style must satisfy.
>
> **Read when:** you create a style, modify tokens or component CSS, update widgets or pre-paint splash colours, check style parity against Classic, support Ultra minimal (`settings.ultraMinimal`), or add a new feature (page, card, field, modal, dock/tab item or control).
>
> **Related:** [glass-material-update.md](glass-material-update.md), [style-audit.md](style-audit.md), [calculations-colors.md](calculations-colors.md), [widgets.md](widgets.md), [file-organisation.md](file-organisation.md)

## The nine styles

| Style | Visual language & typography | Cards, borders & elevation | Trackers, Stats, Period & Custom fields |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Classic** (baseline) | Warm oat & soft pastels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). | 24 px cards, 1 px `--border`, flat cards (no card shadows; soft elevation only on floating dock/modals/popovers/toasts), full-pill nav / dock. | Meals neutral `--surface`; trackers, Poop/Water/Custom stats, Period cards & all 7 Custom sections use `@theme` pastel tokens. |
| **Angular** | Tactical chamfered polygons (`Chakra Petch`). | `0px` radius + `--clip-lg/md/sm/xs/dock` chamfers, borderless, `--card-shadow: none`. | Full-field tactical tones (`--tone-*` / `--tone-*-in`); meals stay neutral. |
| **Aurora** | Frosted glass on lavender-violet gradient + mint / coral heroes (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). | 22 px cards, translucent surfaces with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, soft shadows, opaque `prefers-reduced-transparency` fallbacks. | Full-field frosted translucent tones (`rgba`) with opaque fallbacks under reduced transparency. |
| **Pop** | Neo-brutalist bold solids (`Space Grotesk`). | 20 px cards, thick `2px / 2.5px` borders, hard offset shadows (`#000` light, `#fde047` dark & lite-dark). | Full-field vibrant pop tones keeping thick outlines and offset shadows. |
| **Playful** | Soft bubbly indigo / pastel surfaces (`Space Grotesk`). | 24 px cards, subtle 1 px borders across all 3 modes, soft shadows, rounded 32 / 40 px nav & dock. | Full-field soft bubbly pastel tones; meals stay neutral. |
| **Minimal** | Hairline crisp slate (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). | 14 px cards, flat `1px solid` borders, `--card-shadow: none`, 11 px rounded-square dock, Body & Settings badges. | **Text & icons only:** fields stay neutral `--surface`; only header text & icons take `--tone-ink-*` (`>= 4.5:1` AA). |
| **Mono** | Dynamic single-hue tonal palette across 10 hues (`Space Grotesk`, `src/utils/themes.ts`). | 18 px cards, `1.5px solid` hue-tinted borders, flat, uniform dock, Body & Settings badge plates. | Single-hue stepped tonal washes (`toneBase` / `toneIn`) distinct from neutral meal cards. |
| **Glass** | Scroll-smooth iOS Liquid Glass: balanced frosted crystal panels on pearl-white cool mist (`#eef2fa`) + specular sheen (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). | 26 px cards, multi-rim specular highlight (`--card-shadow` + `--glass-sheen`); live `blur(14px) saturate(160%)` reserved for floating chrome (header, nav, dock, dialogs) + GPU-promoted `body::before` backdrop for zero scroll lag; 16 px ingredient containers. | Balanced crystal washes (`0.64–0.66` alpha light, `0.66–0.68` smoked dark surfaces) with high-contrast inner cards. |
| **Material** | Material You / M3 flat tonal containers (`Roboto` fallback `Plus Jakarta Sans`). | 24 px flat borderless cards, **concentric squircles** (`14/16 px` buttons inside `18/22 px` nav pills, docks & widget trays + progressive `corner-shape: squircle`), 8 px chips. | Full-field flat borderless tonal containers (`--tone-*`). |

## Classic style baseline specification

1. **Neutral surfaces & inks (`src/styles/theme-tokens.css`):**
   - **Light:** `--bg #EFE9DD`, `--surface #FBF8F2`, `--inset #EFE9DD`, `--border #E2D9C8`, `--text #24201C`, `--muted #696158`, `--hover #E6DECF`, `--on-text var(--bg)`.
   - **Lite Dark:** `--bg #272A33`, `--surface #323642`, `--inset #292D37`, `--border #484D5C`, `--text #F3F0EA`, `--muted #BBC1D0`, `--hover #414655`.
   - **Dark:** `--bg #13151A`, `--surface #1B1E26`, `--inset #232733`, `--border #2A2F3D`, `--text #EDEBE6`, `--muted #9AA0AE`, `--hover #2C3140`.
2. **Canonical `@theme` pastel families:** `intake` (`#C9EFD3` / `#17432A`), `activity` (`#F6D0D0` / `#702D32`), `carbs` (`#CFE7FE` / `#183F66`), `protein` (`#FAE0B4` / `#7D4E14`), `fat`/`weight` (`#E2D6FF` / `#49346F`), `nutrient` (`#F7E9AA` / `#5D4911`), `period` (`#F4C6D8` / `#6E2440`), `teal` (`#CDEEEA` / `#0F4A47`).
3. **Classic Dark / Lite-Dark rules:**
   - Never place light-mode dark-pastel ink (`text-[#174A2B]`, `text-[#6E2440]`, `text-[#1A5632]`) directly on `--surface` or `--bg`; use `--intake-on-surface`, `--activity-on-surface`, `--weight-on-surface`, `--text-neg`, `--text-warn`, or `--text-heart`.
   - Never put `border-[var(--border)]` on Classic pastel cards (use pastel-matched `border-*-border` or borderless; non-Classic styles apply `--card-border` via CSS).
   - Every `:hover` rule on a dark-adapted surface must use `var(--hover)`, never `#fffaf0` or `#ffffffa0`.
4. **Canonical geometry, buttons & typography:**
   - Top-level cards: `rounded-3xl` (24 px), `p-4 sm:p-5` (`p-3` Ultra minimal), `space-y-4` page rhythm (`space-y-2.5` ultra). Nested sub-cards/rows: `rounded-2xl` (16 px), `bg-[var(--inset)] border border-[var(--border)]`.
   - Primary CTA: `bg-intake hover:bg-intake-hover text-intake-ink rounded-2xl py-3 text-sm font-extrabold` placed in `Modal.footer`. Destructive text on `--surface`/`--bg`: `text-[var(--text-warn)]`.
   - Icon buttons: `size-11` header, `size-10` modal header, `size-8` row/card actions, `size-7` inline clear.
   - Type: minimum `11px` labels (`text-[11px] font-extrabold uppercase tracking-wider text-[var(--muted)]` eyebrows, `text-xs font-bold text-[var(--muted)]` field labels), weights `500/600/700/800` only, mandatory `tabular-nums` on dynamic numbers.

## Mandatory requirements for every style

1. **Three modes + opaque `--bg` hex:** define `light`, `lite-dark` and `dark` blocks with an opaque `#rrggbb` `--bg` (required by `scripts/generate-splash-themes.mjs` and `index.html` `BG_MAP` so launch has zero flash).
2. **Core token contract:** define surfaces, ink, borders, shadows, radii, clip-paths, energy cards (`--intake-*`, `--burned-*`, `--intake-badge-*`, `--badge-*`), macros & pills (`--carbs/protein/fat-*`, `--pill-c/p/f-*`), nutrients (`--fiber/salt/sugar-*`), `--water-fill` (explicit in all 3 modes), dock plates (`--dock-1..6`, `--dock-icon-1..6`), and Light-mode `--color-*-accent` surface accents. Whenever `--nav-active-bg` is a `linear-gradient(...)`, define a solid `<color>` token `--nav-active-accent` and never pass gradient tokens into `border-color` or `color-mix()`. Never set `--micro-radius: 999px` (max `20px`).
3. **Trackers, Stats, Period & Custom sections (`themed-tones.css`):** meals stay neutral `--surface`; Today trackers (`activity`, `water`, `poop`, `custom`), Statistics cards (`poop`, `water`, `custom`), Period summary cards (`cycle`, `phase`, `next`, `ovulation`) and all 7 Custom sections must define either all 7 `--tone-*` / `--tone-*-in` + `--tone-pale` pairs (full-field styles) or all 7 `--tone-ink-*` tokens (text/icon-only styles like Minimal). Reusable/new tinted cards can use `.kc-themed-card` + `data-kc-tone="diary|meals|recipes|planner|photo|foods|teal"` (with `data-kc-tone-ink` on header text/icons for Minimal).
4. **Controls, icons & dialogs coherence:** the continuous page pill (`.kc-following-indicator`), Today / Period / Body docks (`[data-dock-action]`), Settings badges & switches (`[data-settings-badge]`, `[data-settings-switch]`), Body measurement icon badges (`[data-body-icon]`), Period header icons (`.kc-period-header-icon`), Calendar controls (`[data-cal-*]`), Compact / Normal / Expanded & Stats/Body range switchers (`[data-layout-switch]`, `[data-range-switch]`), Statistics pills/bars (`[data-stat-pill]`, `[data-stat-bar-*]`), and Meal planner / Shopping list controls must all follow the active style.
5. **Widgets (browser gallery + native Android):** Small & Extra-small trays follow button geometry (`widget-preview.css` & `applyTrayShape`); shortcut buttons match `--dock-1..6` (`renderStyledActionBitmap`); **Large & Medium widgets** match the active style's surface, Intake / Burned / Macro cards, badges, icons and progress bars (`.widget-large` in `widget-preview.css` and `applyLargeTheme` in `JournalWidgetProvider.java`).
6. **Accessibility & contrast:** maintain `>= 4.5:1` WCAG AA contrast for `--text` and `--muted` on `--bg`, `--surface` and every `--tone-*`, for `--*-on-surface` on `--surface`, for Minimal `--tone-ink-*` on both `--surface` and `--inset`, and for dynamic Mono inks (`getBestContrastText` / Android `contrastText` must compare exact WCAG contrast ratios around `L ≈ 0.179`, never `0.42`) across all 10 Mono hues × 3 modes, and `>= 3.0:1` for `--water-fill` on its track. Always use `--on-text` (never `--surface`) for text on `var(--text)`. Translucent styles (Aurora, Glass) must provide opaque `#rrggbb` `@media (prefers-reduced-transparency: reduce)` fallbacks for every translucent token.
7. **Wiring & automated verification:** register in `src/types/index.ts`, `src/utils/themes.ts`, `src/components/common/GoalBadge.tsx`, `index.html` `BG_MAP`, `scripts/generate-splash-themes.mjs`, `JournalDevicePlugin.java`, `JournalWidgetProvider.java`, and cover in `tests/themes.test.ts`.

## When adding a new feature (page, card, field, modal or control)

Every new UI surface or control must work cleanly across all **9 styles** and **3 modes** (`light`, `lite-dark`, `dark`) from day one:

1. **No raw colours or hardcoded fonts:** never use raw Tailwind palette classes (`bg-slate-*`, `text-amber-*`, etc.) or arbitrary Tailwind hex colour classes (`bg-[#...]`, `text-[#...]`, `border-[#...]`) in components (blocked by `.agents/hooks/guard-edits.mjs` and `tests/invariants.test.ts`), raw inline hex colours that ignore dark/style modes, or hardcoded `font-family`. Always use `@theme` utilities (`bg-intake`, `text-carbs-ink`, etc.) and Kakcal semantic CSS tokens (`--bg`, `--surface`, `--inset`, `--hover`, `--border`, `--text`, `--muted`, `--on-text`, `--accent-*`, `--intake-*`, `--burned-*`, `--carbs/protein/fat-*`, `--water-fill`).
2. **Neutral pages, cards & nested fields:** give top-level cards/sections `.kc-themed-card` and `rounded-3xl` (or `rounded-2xl`) so `themed-cards.css`, `themed-overrides.css`, `themed-glass.css` and `themed-material.css` apply borders, shadows, radii, Angular chamfers, Glass sheen and Material flat containers automatically. Use `--inset` + `1px solid var(--border)` for nested rows/sub-cards (or reuse `[data-library-card]` / `.journal-*`). If you introduce custom card/control classes, add them to `themed-glass.css` (specular `--glass-sheen` on cards), `themed-material.css` (`border-color: transparent !important` for filled-tonal controls without outlines, plus `@supports (corner-shape: squircle)` for buttons/pills/trays), and `themed-overrides.css` if needed.
3. **Tinted cards, trackers & fields:** reuse an existing semantic hook (`[data-tracker]`, `[data-library-section]`, `[data-stats-tone]`, `[data-period-card]`) or `.kc-themed-card` + `data-kc-tone="diary|meals|recipes|planner|photo|foods|teal"`. **Always** mark header text and icons inside `[data-kc-tone]` with `data-kc-tone-ink` so **Minimal** keeps neutral `--surface` fields and tints only the header (`>= 4.5:1` AA; enforced by `guard-edits.mjs` and `tests/invariants.test.ts`). Because `themed-tones.css` applies `html[data-style]:not([data-style="classic"])`, also ensure **Classic** renders cleanly across Light / Lite Dark / Dark (via tokens or explicit Classic surface/pastel rules).
4. **Controls, inputs, pills, badges & modals:** reuse `Modal` (with `onBack` & sticky `footer`), `Switch` / `SwitchVisual`, `SearchInput`, `SegmentedControl`, `MealSlotPicker`, `AmountStepper`, `CollapseArrow` / `CollapseBody`, `.pressable`, `[data-dock-action]`, `[data-slot-chip]`, `[data-settings-badge]`, `[data-settings-switch]`, `[data-body-icon]`, `[data-layout-switch]`, `[data-range-switch]`, `[data-stat-pill]` and `[data-cal-*]`. Never give tall or multi-line containers `--pill-radius` (`999px` in Aurora and Glass causes semicircular clipping; see `themed-overrides.css`). Use `--hover`, `--text` + `--on-text`, `--nav-active-*` or `--pill-*` for interactive/selected states.
5. **New pages, tabs & dock shortcuts:** wire tabs/pages into `Tabs.tsx` / `PageSwiper.tsx` and dock actions into `ActionDock.tsx` using `--nav-*` and `--dock-*` tokens (or semantic `[data-dock-action]`). Keep `main section.rounded-3xl` / `main div.rounded-3xl` structure so style overrides apply automatically, and check Glass floating header/backdrop layers and Angular `clip-path` bounds.
6. **Support Ultra minimal (`settings.ultraMinimal`):** tighten spacing/padding, collapse redundant labels/units to icons or short forms while preserving `sr-only` / `aria-label` / `title`, and follow the rules below.
7. **Verify across styles & modes:** maintain WCAG AA contrast (`>= 4.5:1` text/muted/ink, `>= 3.0:1` fills), run `npx vitest run tests/themes.test.ts tests/invariants.test.ts`, and inspect UI changes across the **6-state canary matrix** (`Classic · Light`, `Classic · Dark` — plus `Classic · Lite Dark` when touching borders/nested cards — `Glass · Light`, `Pop · Dark`, `Angular · Lite Dark`, `Minimal · Dark`, plus `Mono` and `Material` when adding tinted sections or chips) and with **Ultra minimal** on.

## Ultra minimal mode (`settings.ultraMinimal`)

**Ultra minimal** is an orthogonal density and label-reduction mode toggled in **Settings > Appearance** (`settings.ultraMinimal`). It applies across all 9 theme styles and 3 colour modes:

1. **Collapse labels to icons while keeping accessibility:** replace card headings, secondary subtitles, tab labels (`.kc-following-tab.is-ultra` in `Tabs.tsx`), action labels (`Log`, `Today`), and `Custom` date-range text (`CalendarDays`) with compact icons or short codes (e.g. `ArrowDownLeft` / `Flame` on energy cards, `7d` / `30d`, `L` / `R` on paired Body chips). Always keep a `sr-only` span, `aria-label`, and `title` tooltip so screen readers and hover hints remain intact.
2. **Strip redundant display units:** omit `kcal`, `g`, `ml`, item units, and custom tracker units in display cards, pills, and summaries (`FoodMacroGrid mode="minimal"`, `MealNutrientTotals` `showUnit = false`). Keep units inside interactive inputs and amount editors. In **Statistics**, macro/nutrient average cards replace `g` with `/day` beside the value, and custom tracker charts place `/day` in the bottom-right corner.
3. **Tighten spacing & adapt page layouts:** use compact padding and gaps (`p-2.5`–`p-3.5`, `space-y-2.5`–`space-y-3`). On **Today**, force `Compact` meal layout and hide the layout switcher. On **Custom**, use `.journal-minimal-grid` + `data-section-open` (collapsed sections sit two per row at 50% width; expanded sections span 100%, with `LibrarySectionHeader` hiding overflowing header actions via `useFitActions`) and set `data-minimal` on `JournalSection`.
4. **Never hide essential identity data or functionality:** keep primary numbers, food/item names, meal titles, custom tracker/body-measure names, and expanded activity names visible; never omit logged attributes without a visual indicator (e.g. Poop size bars), never permanently disable expanded section search, and never hardcode English `'/day'` or `'avg'` (use `t.stats.perDay` / localized strings). Every top-level page (`Today`, `Fasting`, `Custom`, `Period`, `Body`, `Statistics`, `FoodDatabase`) must handle `settings.ultraMinimal`.
