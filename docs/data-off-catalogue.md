# Open Food Facts offline catalogue

> **Scope:** 130,000-product KOFF v1 binary: contents, format, search, sugar handling, rebuild steps, licence, Browse Foods.
>
> **Read when:** you touch `off-foods.bin`, `offCatalog.ts`, `useOffCatalog.ts`, `build-off-catalog.mjs` or food search ranking.
>
> **Search runs in a Web Worker** (`src/workers/offCatalogSearch.worker.ts`, client `src/utils/catalogueSearch.ts`) for Add food, the planner and the Custom / Settings pickers; the worker parses its own copy of the file with the same reader (`parseOffCatalog`). The food database page and barcode lookup still use the main-thread copy. See [search-and-animation-performance.md](search-and-animation-performance.md).
>
> **Related:** data-nutrition.md, storage-and-privacy.md, file-map/data.md

**Browse the full database:** Custom > Foods > Browse Foods opens the same offline index as Add Food. Its count includes matching catalogue and local foods without counting remembered barcodes twice. Name, brand, barcode and category searches cover the whole file, not just the current page. Pages contain at most 36 decoded food records from the combined results. Favourites and custom foods retain their own filters. If the file fails to load, the browser identifies the partial list and offers a retry. Restore `public/data/off-foods.bin` from Git if it is missing. `scripts/prepare-android.mjs` copies the file into `dist/data/` (and so into the APK) and refuses to continue if its KOFF magic number is wrong.

`public/data/off-foods.bin` (12.3 MB, 130,000 products) is the offline product catalogue. It is built from the [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org) CSV export (about 3.2 million of its 4.9 million products have a complete nutrition table) by `scripts/build-off-catalog.mjs`, run with `node scripts/build-off-catalog.mjs`.

- **Kept:** a name, a barcode and a complete, self-consistent per-100 g/ml table (energy, fat, carbohydrates, protein; the energy must agree with the macros within a generous tolerance, alcohol included). **Ranked:** Italian market first, then popularity and completeness. The best 130,000 are written (all of them are Italian-market products), after removing duplicates (same name, brand and energy).
- **Saved per product:** name, brand, barcode, kcal, carbs, **sugar**, fat, protein, fibre, salt, g or ml, category, and the **classic portion** (Open Food Facts `serving_quantity`) when the record has one: 128,697 products have sugar and 25,758 have a classic portion. Missing values are stored as unknown, never invented.
- **Sugar** is stored in `FoodItem.sugar` and snapshotted on every logged item. **Settings > Today & pages > Track sugar intake** adds it to Today beside salt, food search and quantity screens, custom-food creation, barcode results, saved meals/recipes, day overviews, Statistics averages and the daily breakdown. Products without a sugar value show `—` in food details; daily totals sum the available values. The 410 bundled foods have no sugar value.
- **Format "KOFF" v1**, chosen for speed: little-endian, every section 8-byte aligned, so the browser wraps the downloaded buffer in typed arrays with no parsing or copying (barcodes as `Float64Array`, nutrients as scaled `Uint16Array`, string offsets as `Uint32Array`). Names and brands are UTF-8 blobs decoded only for the rows a search returns; one ASCII blob (`" word word\n"` per product) is the search index. The layout is documented at the top of the script and read by `src/data/offCatalog.ts`.
- **Search:** word-prefix match over name and brand, every word must match, whole-phrase and leading-word hits first, popularity order inside a group. A search takes about 50 ms on 130,000 products. Hits are ranked on the same scale as the bundled fuzzy search (above their typo matches, below an exact word) and mixed into the same list. Products you pick or star are copied into `kcalsync_used_foods` so they stay resolvable by id.
- **Rebuild:** `node scripts/build-off-catalog.mjs` streams the 1.5 GB export from `static.openfoodfacts.org` (nothing is stored; about 1 GB of RAM is enough; around two minutes), or `node scripts/build-off-catalog.mjs <export.csv.gz> --limit 130000 --out public/data/off-foods.bin` for a local file. Afterwards bump `DATA_CACHE` in `public/offline-worker.js`, then `node scripts/prepare-android.mjs`.
