# Home-screen widgets (Android + browser gallery)

> **Scope:** Four native widgets (Small, Extra-small, Medium, Large), their styles, the custom button, icon rendering rules and RemoteViews restrictions.
>
> **Read when:** you touch `JournalWidgetProvider`, widget layouts/drawables, `WidgetGallery.tsx` or widget settings.
>
> **Related:** file-map/android-native.md, file-map/tour-widgets-intro.md, ../android/BUILD_APK.md

| Size | Actions and information |
| --- | --- |
| Small (primary) | Five circular shortcuts: Food, Movement, Water, Poop and Weight, plus an optional sixth **Custom** button. |
| Extra-small | Three circular shortcuts: Food, Water and Poop. |
| Medium | **Default size 4x2** (`widget_medium_info.xml`: `minHeight` and `minResizeHeight` 110 dp, `targetCellHeight` 2; it can still be resized taller). Same figures as the Large widget in a shorter layout (`widget_medium*.xml`, `widget_actions_five_compact.xml`). **Vertical budget:** the **macro row (carbs, protein, fat; grams only, 12 sp) is exactly as tall as the buttons (40 dp)**, the five buttons are 40 dp, and the **intake and burned cards take all the remaining height** (`layout_weight` 1), so they are always the biggest items: **no icon** (the intake / burned cards show only the value, centred; the icons were removed from `widget_medium.xml`, `widget_medium_dark.xml` and the gallery preview, the Large widget keeps them), value 24 sp auto-sized between 12 and 26 sp on Android 8+ (`WidgetValueRow`), balance badge on the same line, **no progress bars**. The optional **Custom** button is the sixth button. Fixed part: 4 dp padding + 3 + 3 dp margins + 40 + 40 dp = 94 dp. |
| Large | Intake and burned cards (kcal, with the balance badge and a progress bar), carbs, protein and fat with a **macro letter in the top-left corner** (C, P, F; G in Italian), grams and share of calories, and the five shortcuts (plus the optional **Custom** button). Same arrangement as Ultra minimal Today. |

**Style and Custom button** (Settings > Appearance > Home-screen widgets, and Settings > Custom buttons): *Light* is the default; *Dark* keeps the translucent background, just darker (all four widgets, with light-on-dark text and tinted cards on the Large and Medium ones). Switching the **app** to Dark also sets the widgets to Dark, and they can still be changed independently. **The widget button style matches the active theme style** (`classic`, `angular`, `aurora`, `pop`, `playful`, `minimal`, `mono`): button geometry (`--dock-btn-radius`, `--clip-sm` chamfered corners in `angular`), borders (`--dock-btn-border`), fills (`--dock-1..6`) and icon inks (`--dock-icon-1..6`) follow the chosen style preset in both the browser widget gallery and the native Android widgets (`themeStyle` and `monoHue` in `publishNutrition`). The *Custom button in widgets* switch adds the sixth custom-tracker button to the **Small, Medium and Large** widgets (not Extra-small); it does what the Today dock's custom button does: it opens the only custom tracker, or the picker when there are several. The choices travel to the launcher in `publishNutrition` (`widgetStyle`, `themeStyle`, `monoHue`, `widgetCustomButton`), the widgets re-render immediately, and the layout of each style is a separate `*_dark` resource because RemoteViews cannot recolour a layout at runtime. The browser tour's widget gallery reads the same settings.

**Icons in non-classic styles.** In every style other than `classic` the native widgets draw each shortcut into a bitmap (`renderStyledActionBitmap` in `JournalWidgetProvider.java`): the style's plate (circle, chamfered, rounded square, border), then the icon glyph tinted with the style's ink colour. The glyph now comes from dedicated plate-less vector drawables (`res/drawable/widget_glyph_{food,movement,water,poop,weight,custom}.xml`, drawn at the same 24 of 48 units as the classic icons and tinted with `Drawable.setTint`). Previously the glyph was cut out of the circular classic icon by colour distance against a sample pixel that lay *outside* the circle, so with any other style the icons came out as solid ink blobs or vanished. The classic `widget_*_icon.xml` drawables (plate + glyph) are unchanged and still used for the `classic` style and as the fallback. When you edit an icon, keep the matching `widget_glyph_*` file in step.

**Pill follows the buttons.** The translucent tray (pill) behind the Small and Extra-small buttons matches the button shape of the active style, concentric with the buttons plus the tray padding. Round styles (`classic`, `aurora`, `pop`, `playful`) keep the full pill; `minimal` (rounded-square buttons) and `mono` get a rounded rectangle (15 / 16 dp natively, 19 / 20 px in the gallery); `angular` (chamfered buttons) gets a chamfered tray (12 dp native chamfer from a generated 9-patch, 14 px in the gallery, where the border and shadow are dropped because the clip would cut them). Native: `applyTrayShape` in `JournalWidgetProvider.java` swaps the `@+id/widget_tray` background with `setBackgroundResource` and restores the 4 dp padding (a background swap resets padding). Browser: `--widget-pill-radius` / `--widget-pill-clip` in `widget-preview.css`. If a style's button radius changes, change its tray too.

**Glass and Material styles.** `glass` draws tinted plates with a white rim (`renderStyledActionBitmap`; the tray stays the round translucent pill, with a frosted blur in the gallery); `material` draws tonal rounded squares and swaps the tray for `widget_tray_material[_dark].xml` (20 dp radius natively, 24 px in the gallery via `--widget-pill-radius`). Colours mirror `--dock-1..6` / `--dock-icon-1..6` in `src/styles/theme-glass.css` and `theme-material.css`; the Java whitelist is in `JournalDevicePlugin.publishNutrition`. See [glass-material-update.md](glass-material-update.md).

All action buttons are **icon-only** and retain accessible names. The two smaller widgets have a translucent background and no branding or headings. The Large widget has no logo, name or "today" label. Its intake and burned cards use the app's Today colours, icons and badge rules, and the macro cards show grams with their share of calories. The full wordmark appears only in page headers. Labels outside the widget bodies identify sizes in the tour or the launcher picker.

The guided tour opens real dialogs in its same-origin app frame, and each page step sets the journal to that page. The native widgets are `AppWidgetProvider`/`RemoteViews` implementations (`JournalWidgetProvider.Small`, `.Essential`, `.Medium`, `.Large`) with offline vector drawables and immutable intents. **Widget layouts may only use `@RemoteView` classes** (`FrameLayout`, `LinearLayout`, `RelativeLayout`, `TextView`, `ImageView`, `ImageButton`, `ProgressBar`, …): a plain `<View>` spacer makes the launcher refuse the whole widget with "Can't load widget" / "Impossibile caricare il widget", which is why the Large widget's header spacers are empty `FrameLayout`s. Both target **today** even if the journal was browsing a historical date. Tapping opens a form; only Save writes an entry. Native nutrition is a derived private cache updated on edits/import, not a second journal. Missing days render zero. The system can defer periodic and midnight refreshes. Widget launcher behavior must be checked on the target device.

**Minimal and Playful button parity.** In `minimal` Movement uses a neutral plate with a rose icon (`#fda4af` dark / `#9f1239` light) and Food uses emerald ink (`#34d399` dark / `#15803d` light) so Food and Water are distinct in both the app dock and `renderStyledActionBitmap`. In `playful`, `renderStyledActionBitmap` now switches to the Dark palette (`#065f46`, `#9f1239`, `#1e3a8a`, `#854d0e`, `#4c1d95`, `#854d0e`) when dark widgets are enabled.

**Mono style, Food and Movement buttons.** In `mono` the Food and Movement buttons used to use the stronger intake / burned colours while the other four used the soft card colour. All six buttons now share one plate and one ink: `--dock-1..6` / `--dock-icon-1..6` in `getMonoVariables` (`src/utils/themes.ts`) are the card colours, which also feeds the widget gallery, and `renderStyledActionBitmap` (`JournalWidgetProvider.java`) uses the same single fill for every `index` in `mono`.

**Minimal style, pill (tray).** In `minimal` the tray behind the Small and Extra-small buttons is no longer the soft translucent cream pill of the round styles. It is a crisp slate surface with a hairline border and the style's 16 px dock radius, like the minimal dock and cards: Light = `#F2EEF2F6` with a 1 dp `#CBD5E1` border, Dark = `#F20D1320` with a 1 dp `#1E293B` border (`res/drawable/widget_tray_minimal.xml` / `_dark.xml`; gallery: `html[data-style='minimal'] .quick-widget` in `widget-preview.css`, no shadow or blur). The surplus pill on the gallery's Intake card uses the intake palette (see [calculations-colors.md](calculations-colors.md)).
